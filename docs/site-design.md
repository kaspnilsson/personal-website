# kasptrax.com design system

kasptrax.com uses the renders.nyc chassis directly: the same fonts, tokens, components and measurements, with release artwork as the only colour. The site carries no press or promo photographs.

Casa and Fieldnotes are purpose-built shells on the same chassis, like the Renders SMS and legal routes.

## The sheet

The public pages (home, about, 404, error) sit on a paper sheet defined in `src/app/sheet.css`, applied by a `.sheet` wrapper. It carries over what the Renders poster hill climb learned (renders `drafts/design/hillclimb-2026-11-03`: `brand-direction.md`, `rubric-v4.txt`, `shared/master.py`), and was then climbed against a blind council (Opus, GPT Astra, Gemini Neon) on a four-line rubric: presence, wholeness, material, ease. What the climb settled:

- Paper `#e4e0d6` and ink `#121110`: black, paper and earth only, never pure digital values.
- The opening is one spread. The latest cover is crushed against the left edge and printed into the paper (`mix-blend-mode: multiply`); its column holds everything else on the cover's axes: the release in display type, one solid Listen action, the bio and bookings, the rest of the catalogue with covers, and the profiles.
- The close repeats the spread: the mastered photograph (`assets/kasp/printed/night-wall.jpg`, run through `master.py` until it is ink on this paper) bleeds off the left edge with UPDATES on its top edge and the signup on its bottom edge. On phones the two stack.
- Display type (release titles, UPDATES) and the two action buttons are printed through the `#ink` and `#ink-solid` SVG filters in `site-footer.tsx`: toner density and a few pinholes, with letter shapes intact.
- One surface: `scripts/make-surface.py` bakes the master bus's grain, specks and room into small tiles in the paper itself. Two grain tiles of unrelated sizes are layered so the grain never visibly repeats. They sit under the content, so nothing a visitor reads is flecked.
- Usability wins over the flyer, and this is a hard rule: anything a visitor reads stays clean and whole, 12 px or larger, and is never worn, knocked out or cropped. The council read-back (transcribing the title, date, UPDATES and the Subscribe label from actual-size phone captures) is how that was checked.

Every public page shares this design, not just the paper. Home, About, Thoughts, 404 and the error page all open with the same spread (`src/components/spread.tsx`, the `.spread` rules in `sheet.css`): a printed image crushed against the left edge with the page's column on its axes, the page title in printed display type (`.sheet-display`) and the page's main action as a solid printed button. About uses the mastered light-trails portrait (`assets/kasp/printed/light-trails.jpg`); Thoughts, 404 and error use person-free details of the same graffiti wall (`wall-loops.jpg`, `wall-letters.jpg`), so press photos stay at two.

The Updates signup is for the music only, so it appears on the homepage alone (`<SiteFooter signup />`). Every other page closes on the ink footer.

Casa and Fieldnotes keep the dark Renders chassis.

## Thoughts

`/thoughts` is the blog: the index is a spread whose column lists the posts the way the homepage column lists records, and each post sets the spread for long reading, with a details rail on the left axis and one reading column. Posts are listed in `src/content/thoughts/posts.ts`; each body is trusted HTML at `src/content/thoughts/<slug>/body.html`, with images in `public/assets/thoughts/<slug>/`. The feed is `/thoughts/rss.xml`, and posts are in the sitemap.

- Titles are Gibson Bold uppercase. Body text is JetBrains Mono at 15px (14.5px on phones) with a 66ch measure, the size of the About bio, so long reading stays comfortable. Labels, dates, captions and tables stay at 11 to 13px mono.
- Figures inside posts use the ink palette plus one earth tone, `#8a3a1c`, where a chart needs to separate two series. Screenshots print into the paper with `mix-blend-mode: multiply`, like the covers.
- Wide tables scroll inside their own container; the page never scrolls sideways.

## Rules

- Dark chassis (Casa, Fieldnotes): ground `#0a0a0a`, ink `#e5e5e5`, rules `#262626` and `#3a3a3a`. Pure white only on hover. No accent colours, gradients, radius or drop shadows in the interface.
- Gibson Bold, uppercase, for headings and titles. JetBrains Mono for facts, labels, body and actions.
- Motion is functional: transforms and opacity only, power-curve easing, reduced motion respected.
- Show facts at rest. No placeholder releases, events or sets.

## Adding a release

Add an entry to `src/data/releases.ts` with its public links and cover, and add the cover to `public/assets/kasp/releases`. The homepage lists releases newest first. Keep unreleased plans out of the file until they have public links.

## Private routes

`/casa` and `/fieldnotes` never appear in public navigation, the sitemap or robots rules. Casa sets `noindex` metadata; Fieldnotes sends `X-Robots-Tag` and private cache headers. Fieldnotes' UI files live in `src/lib/fieldnotes/assets.json`.
